## Proven facts

For the tested Linux RPDB lowering:

- `iif lo` selects actual host-originated traffic for helper dispatch;
- `not iif lo` selects forwarded non-local ingress while host-originated traffic falls
  through to the ordinary host default;
- `iif <exact-interface>` scopes helper dispatch to that ingress and leaves another ingress
  on the ordinary default path;
- multiple selector-specific dispatch rules form the expected union;
- an unconditional dispatch provides the `:any` behavior;
- the higher-priority `iif <managed-boundary> lookup main` rule prevents STACK-origin
  traffic from being captured by `:external` or `:any`;
- `lookup main suppress_prefixlength 0` preserves an explicit administrator non-default
  route before selector dispatch;
- the global preserve-main lookup plus a selector that does not match does not alter the
  eventual ordinary default route;
- the experiment did not modify ordinary `main` routes.
