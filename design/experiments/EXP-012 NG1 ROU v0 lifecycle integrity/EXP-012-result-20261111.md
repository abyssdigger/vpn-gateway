## Confirmed behavior

The live experiment confirms the complete v0 control-loop boundary:

1. ROU creates and tears down its private namespace/veth appliance.
2. Real ocserv runs inside that namespace while the unchanged external endpoint remains
   published from the main namespace.
3. Two simultaneous real OpenConnect clients establish DTLS through independent UDP peer
   associations.
4. Two concrete `vpnsXX` bindings are admitted with deterministic metrics 10 and 20.
5. Real payload reaches the protected destination through the preferred binding.
6. Preferred-binding disappearance preserves the survivor metric and payload fails over.
7. A reconnected fresh binding returns without survivor metric churn.
8. Zero admitted bindings retain protected and terminal blackholes and payload remains
   fail-closed.
9. Recoverable owned route and firewall drift is repaired under an observable
   `net.ipv4.ip_forward=0` barrier; forwarding is restored only after verification.
10. Structural loss of `host0` causes non-zero controller exit and complete cleanup of
    namespace/veth/publication runtime state.

## Corrections found during the live gate

Three defects were exposed before the final PASS and corrected without weakening any
hypothesis:

- the first flush of a not-yet-created profile table is a valid empty bootstrap state;
  only the specific kernel `FIB table does not exist` response is tolerated;
- ocserv sec-mod chroot semantics require the internal `socket-file` to use a flat
  chroot-visible `/run` path; the original nested runtime path was invalid;
- H8 originally attempted to delete the managed route from `main`; the harness was
  corrected to delete it explicitly from profile table 100.
