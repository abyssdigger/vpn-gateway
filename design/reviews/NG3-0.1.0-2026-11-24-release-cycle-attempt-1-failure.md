# NG3 0.1.0 — release-cycle attempt 1 failure

**Release:** `0.1.0`  
**Failed candidate:** `release/0.1.0` at
`7cb662055a898d8a8aa0dce0163259e28e896bd7`  
**Disposition:** **FAILED RELEASE-CYCLE ATTEMPT**

## Failure reason

The first 0.1.0 release-preparation attempt proceeded through bundle QA, requirements coverage,
candidate freeze and fulfillment review **without a previously approved release-specific
projectholder scope contract**.

The omission mattered materially.

The normative Constraint `VPNG-U004-R5.C1` permits a first release without Protective mode,
but does not require that omission. The projectholder had separately decided that Release-1 /
0.1.0 must include Protective mode in full. That stronger, non-contradictory release-specific
decision had no project artifact in which it was recorded.

As a result, the attempt incorrectly treated the three known A.011 ProtectiveModeManager
`partial` edges as non-blocking and produced a PASS readiness verdict against a weaker scope
than the projectholder had actually authorized.

## Evidence disposition

The attempt's QA, artifacts, REQCOV and fulfillment reviews remain valid historical evidence for
the state they actually checked. They are not erased or rewritten.

However, their **release-readiness verdict is not valid for publication of 0.1.0** because the
candidate was not checked against the approved projectholder scope.

No `v0.1.0` release tag or actual release publication was created from the failed attempt.

## Corrective action

The release methodology now requires an approved
`release/<version>/RELEASE-SCOPE.md` before release-publication preparation may begin.

For 0.1.0 the approved scope explicitly requires full `VPNG-U004-R5*` Protective mode and
does not exercise the omission permitted by `VPNG-U004-R5.C1`.

The next release attempt is blocked until the missing Protective mode materialization/evidence is
completed and a new REQCOV/QA/fulfillment sequence is performed against that approved scope.
